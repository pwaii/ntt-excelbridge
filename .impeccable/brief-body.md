## Direction contract

THESIS: ExcelBridge is an auditor's working paper, not a dashboard. Every
proposed change is a marked-up line tied to its evidence and a sign-off; the
two mandatory review gates are two sign-offs on the paper. It refuses the
generic SaaS "cards on a grey ground" arrangement and the literal
spreadsheet-grid cliché (the incumbent "framed sheet").

OWN-WORLD: Warm working-paper ground (uncoated buff/cream), ink near-black.
Color is quarantined to meaning: audit red for exceptions/reject, audit green
for agreed/accept, one restrained accent for the primary action; the shell
stays neutral. A workhorse serif carries figures and values with tabular
lining numerals; a grotesque carries labels; mono carries IDs, headers,
formulas, and cross-references. Density is a ruled ledger's: hairline rules,
tight rows, right-aligned figures. Signature move: the prepared / reviewed /
approved sign-off rail, and per-finding audit tick marks tying a proposed
value to its before/after evidence.

STORY: A financial reviewer uploads workbooks, sees deterministic findings and
AI proposals as marked-up lines, inspects before/after evidence per item,
records accept/reject/edit/ignore decisions, signs off at each gate, and
downloads a new output plus audit trail. They always know what is a proposal,
a draft, submitted, and applied — and that the source is untouched.

FIRST VIEWPORT: A left sign-off rail naming the workflow's stages as audit
steps (prepared → reviewed → approved), with the current gate marked. The work
area is the working paper: a ruled findings ledger on the left, the selected
finding's before/proposed tie-out with its evidence and tick mark on the right.
The primary action (continue / sign off / submit) sits bottom-right of the
paper. No hero, no decorative chrome.
